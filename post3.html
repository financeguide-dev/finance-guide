Budgeting Basics

Budgeting means planning how you will use your money each month. It helps you control spending and avoid financial problems.

Why budgeting is important

A budget helps you understand your income and expenses clearly so you don’t spend more than you earn.

How to create a simple budget

First, write down your monthly income. Then list all your expenses like rent, food, transport, and bills. Subtract expenses from income to see what is left.

Stick to your plan

The most important part of budgeting is consistency. Follow your plan and adjust it when needed.

Conclusion

Budgeting is a simple but powerful tool that helps you manage your money and reach your financial goals.
